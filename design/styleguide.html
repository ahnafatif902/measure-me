How the estimate is calculated

Body / note — Outfit 400, 14/1.625
text-sm leading-relaxed text-ink/80

Head length is the eye-to-mouth distance ÷ 0.28. Eyes are treated as halfway from crown to chin, and the mouth as 78% of head height. The crown sits half a head above the eyes.

Shape and elevation

One radius family: 24px cards, 16px wells and tiles, fully round pills. Cards carry a 1px ink/10 border and the smallest Tailwind shadow; the photo frame is solid ink.

Card — rounded-3xl

Well — rounded-2xl

16px radius · bg token inside surface cards · px-3 py-3

Components

Buttons — always pills

Base: inline-flex items-center justify-center gap-2 rounded-full font-medium transition-colors duration-200. Sizes: md = min-h-11 px-4 text-sm, lg = min-h-12 w-full px-5 text-base. Disabled is the same button at 45% opacity.

The estimate action is a size-lg primary pill; it is disabled until a photo is present.

Pose-quality meter — h-2, ink/10 track, primary fill

Good pose Pose quality 92 / 100

This scores the pose — standing straight, full body in frame, camera roughly level. It is not an accuracy percentage, and it never means certain. Typical error is several centimeters.

Sliders — native inputs, accent-color primary

Overlay vocabulary

Drawn in screen coordinates over the photo. Marker: r 11 primary circle, 3px surface stroke. Rod: 6px surface halo under a 2px primary line, end caps ±12. Head bracket at +22 from the chin, caps ±8. Labels: Outfit, surface fill with ink stroke via paint-order: stroke — 12px with 3px stroke on markers, 13px with 4px stroke on the rod.
