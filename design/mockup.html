      How the estimate is calculated

      height_cm = (person_pixel_height / head_pixel_height) × average_head_cm

      Standing height starts from the constant 7.5 × head length. Pixel stature is the vertical distance from the crown to the lowest foot point. Head length defaults to 23 cm. Both constants are yours to tune. MediaPipe's 3D pose is not metric, so those units are never treated as centimeters.

      • Eyes are halfway from crown to chin. The mouth is at 78% of head height.
      • Head pixels = eye-to-mouth pixels ÷ 0.28.
      • If the mouth isn't reliable, the nose tip at 63% of head height is used instead.
      • If the face isn't reliable, head pixels = shoulder width ÷ 1.7.
      • If the head still can't be measured, head pixels = body pixels ÷ 7.5, and confidence drops. That fallback mostly repeats the average.
      • Tuned for a standing adult. Children usually need a shorter head length and fewer heads.
