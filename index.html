Place three points

  1. Tap the top of your head — highest point of skull or hair
  2. Tap your chin — bottom of the jaw
  3. Tap the floor at your feet — at the heel or toe, whichever is lowest

Drag any dot to fine-tune — a magnifier follows your finger.

Try this

  • Stand 2.5–3.5 m back so your whole body fits with space above your head and below your feet.
  • Face the camera straight — arms at your sides, feet slightly apart and visible.
  • Avoid long coats or very loose clothing that hides your body shape.
  • Use bright, even light; avoid strong backlight or harsh shadows on your face.
  • Ask someone to take the photo (or use a timer) and hold the phone level at chest height.
